Model appearance

Used as concrete anchors in the prompt sent to Claude, so the generated model reflects these specifics instead of a vague "plus-size" guess. All optional — leave blank to fall back to the generic description.

+50%
Image models tend to under-deliver on extreme body-size instructions. If your weight above is 200 lbs and this is set to +50%, the prompt actually sent to FASHN will say 300 lbs — your saved setting stays the real number, only what's sent to the model is inflated.
Used to check whether a garment will actually fit snugly or loosely on you, based on its own bust measurement (when the product page lists one) — e.g. catches a jacket that will look oversized because the garment's bust measurement is a lot bigger than yours. Optional: if left off, a rough estimate is used instead, based on your cup size above (assuming an average band size — real fit varies more than that alone can capture, so turn this on and enter your real number for a more accurate result).

Your model photo
